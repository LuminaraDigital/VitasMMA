# VitasMMA

**AI MMA Coach for fighters and combat athletes.** VitasMMA is an AI-powered coaching platform that analyzes training footage, maps a fighter's style across striking, grappling, and clinch, and generates personalized training blocks that evolve over time.

## Overview

VitasMMA is built for combat athletes who want more than a generic workout app. The platform combines AI video analysis, Fighter DNA profiling, gamified progression, fight-camp planning, and strength & conditioning programming into one system.

It is designed for:
- Amateur fighters building their fundamentals
- Competitors sharpening their game plan with analytics
- Coaches and fans who want deeper fight IQ and style breakdowns

## Core Features

### Fighter DNA Profiling
- Quantifies core attributes across **Striking**, **Grappling**, and **Clinch**
- Detects training archetypes such as well-rounded fighters, pressure fighters, and submission-focused athletes
- Adapts training plans as the athlete develops

### AI Video Breakdown
- Upload sparring, padwork, bagwork, rolling, or fight footage
- Receive frame-by-frame analysis and actionable technique feedback
- Surface tendencies, strengths, and areas to improve over time

### Multi-Discipline Coaching
Supports a broad range of combat sports disciplines, including:
- Boxing
- Muay Thai
- Kickboxing
- Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)
- Wrestling
- Judo
- Sambo
- MMA hybrid styles

### Fight Camp System
- AI-structured fight camps for technique, sparring prep, tapering, and recovery
- Personalized programming based on goals, available training days, and fighter profile
- Support for both competitive preparation and everyday skill development

### Strength & Conditioning Engine
- Combat-specific programming for hypertrophy, explosive power, and endurance
- S&C archetypes including Balanced Athlete, Power Builder, Endurance Machine, and Explosive Fighter
- Hybrid programming that combines technical training with conditioning

### Gamified Progression
- XP, levels, streaks, and training milestones
- Career-mode inspired progression for long-term consistency
- Community and leaderboard potential for fighter engagement

### Telegram Mini App Experience
- Built to live inside Telegram for lower onboarding friction
- Community access, early beta distribution, and direct user engagement
- Roadmap toward broader Web2.5/Web3 reward mechanics

## Product Vision

VitasMMA aims to become the training companion fighters actually want in their pocket: a platform that understands the language of combat sports and helps athletes improve with structured, personalized feedback.

The long-term vision combines:
- AI coaching for real combat sports development
- Community-driven progression and accountability
- Reward systems layered in gradually without forcing users into crypto from day one

## Who It's For

### Beginners
Fighters starting out can use VitasMMA to get structure, identify technical weaknesses, and build consistent habits with guided progression.

### Amateur Fighters
Athletes preparing for their first fights or local competition can use the platform to organize training blocks, sharpen technique, and track progress.

### Competitors
More advanced fighters can upload footage, analyze patterns, and refine preparation around specific goals, matchups, and performance trends.

### Coaches and Enthusiasts
VitasMMA also supports coaches and serious fans through style breakdowns, fight IQ learning, and performance analysis.
